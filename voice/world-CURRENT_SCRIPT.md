This is the authoritative script for the revised movies, training and daily briefings. Each spoken line is also its exact caption. Dhrubo is one calm, friendly teammate. Use one voice, rate and tone throughout. No character voices, dramatic announcer, spoken stage directions or added words.

The launch movie is a **practice preview**, before planning. Training introduces caring for the habitat. Mars Base introduces choosing a site and managing five days.
